# Crossover Gauntlet
### A Roguelike Final Project

---

## What Is This?

A browser-based roguelike game inspired by *The Binding of Isaac*, as well as several anime and manga series. Fight through a dungeon across 3 floors, defeat 3 unique bosses, and reach the end.

---

## Characters

Choose from 3 playable characters, each with unique stats and attacks:

| Character | Role | Attack |
|-----------|------|--------|
| **Kaido** | High HP, tanky build | Fire beam |
| **Dio** | Low HP, high damage glass cannon | Knife throw |
| **Levi** | High speed | Sword slash |

---

## Powers

At the start of each run you are offered 2 random powers from a pool of 6. Each power is explained in-game with its description and upgrade effect.

Press **F1** on the power select screen to manually choose any of the 6 powers for testing purposes.

> **Note:** Kaido does not benefit from the Spin ability. If you select Kaido and choose Spin via F1, the game will not proceed. Avoid this combination.

---

## How To Play

| Input | Action |
|-------|--------|
| **WASD** | Move |
| **Arrow Keys** | Attack |
| **E** | Activate power *(active powers only)* |
| **ESC** | Pause |

Go through the **green door** on the right to advance between rooms. Fight through enemy rooms until you reach the boss.

After clearing each floor:

- **Floor 1 clear** — Your chosen power evolves into a stronger upgraded form
- **Floor 2 clear** — Your character receives a stat boost specific to their build
- **Floor 3** — Face the final boss, Gojo

---

## Enemies

Enemy difficulty and composition increases each floor:

- **Basic Enemy** — Charges directly at the player and deals contact damage
- **Ranged Enemy** — Keeps its distance and shoots projectiles; watch for the wind-up animation before it fires
- **Tank / Brute** — High HP and slow moving, but periodically charges at the player with a powerful rush attack — watch for the wind-up

## Death Screens

Each way to die has its own unique death message. All death screens can be previewed through the dev door without having to actually die in-game.

---

## Dev Shortcuts

The game includes several developer shortcuts to help you experience everything without replaying from the start:

- **F1 on power select** — Choose any of the 6 powers manually
- **Dev door** *(left side of the starting room)* — Opens a menu with the following options:
  - Skip directly to any boss fight *(upgrades are applied automatically)*
  - Watch the ending cinematic for each character
  - Preview the credits
  - Browse all death screens organized by cause of death

---

## Known Issues

The game may have bugs that weren't caught during solo testing. If anything goes wrong or behaves unexpectedly, refresh the page and start a new run from the beginning.
